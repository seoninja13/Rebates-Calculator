Solar Installation

Federal Solar Tax Credit
Tax credit available for up to 30%

Eligibility: Homeowners and businesses installing solar systems

Requirements: System must meet safety and performance standards

How to Apply: Claim on federal tax return using Form 5695

California Solar Tax Credit
Tax credit available for up to 10%

Eligibility: California residents with solar installations

Requirements: System must be installed by licensed contractor

How to Apply: Claim on state tax return using Form 3528

Alameda County Solar Tax Credit
$5000 Rebate available for PG&E Customers

Eligibility: PG&E customers in Alameda County

Requirements: Minimum 3kW system size

How to Apply: Submit application through PG&E's Solar Rebate Program
